# L03-M06 - VWAP Context: Trend, Balance, and Distance

**Estimated time:** 36 minutes

## Objective
Classify price interaction with VWAP in trending, balanced, and extended conditions without turning distance from the line into an automatic trade.

## Why it matters
The same VWAP location can mean different things in a directional session, a rotating range, or an unusually extended move. Structure and session context determine whether the reference is useful.

## Core ideas
- In a directional session, price may remain mostly on one side of VWAP while pullbacks hold away from or near the line.
- In a balanced session, repeated crossings can show rotation around the weighted average rather than a durable directional edge.
- Distance from VWAP is descriptive unless it is compared with the instrument's own historical behavior and current volatility.
- A VWAP cross or touch does not guarantee mean reversion, continuation, support, or resistance.

## Scenario
US100 remains above session VWAP through the morning, but its distance from the line expands into prior resistance. The context is directional and extended; neither fact authorizes a chase or a blind fade.

## Practice
Review three completed sessions. Label each as directional, balanced, or unclear, then describe VWAP location, crossings, and distance without proposing an entry.

## Knowledge check
- **What can repeated VWAP crossings indicate?** Rotation or balance, subject to structure and session context.
- **Does large distance from VWAP guarantee mean reversion?** No.
- **What makes VWAP distance comparable?** The instrument's historical behavior, session, and current volatility.

## Common mistake
Fading every extended move or trading every VWAP cross without classifying the market condition.

## Risk note
VWAP location and distance are contextual observations, not permission to chase price, fade momentum, or increase leverage.

## Evidence classification
Documented indicator behavior and professional observation
