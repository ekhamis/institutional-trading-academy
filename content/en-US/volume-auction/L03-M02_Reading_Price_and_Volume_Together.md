# L03-M02 - Reading Price and Volume Together

**Estimated time:** 32 minutes

## Objective
Compare trading activity with the resulting price movement instead of interpreting either variable alone.

## Why it matters
The same amount of activity can produce a wide expansion, a small rotation, or no progress. The price result changes the meaning of the observation.

## Core ideas
- Expanding range with rising activity can show participation, but it does not remove risk.
- Heavy activity with little price progress can reflect two-sided trade or opposition and requires more evidence.
- Low activity can reflect weak participation, a quiet session, or limitations in the available feed.
- Compare like with like: the same instrument, venue, timeframe, and relevant session.

## Scenario
Gold advances on rising volume and wide candles, then prints heavy volume with a narrow body at resistance. The later bar shows effort without equal progress and deserves a fresh assessment.

## Practice
Find one example each of high activity with wide progress and high activity with little progress. Describe the difference without predicting the next candle.

## Knowledge check
- **Why compare price with volume?** Activity matters differently depending on the price result.
- **Does heavy activity with little progress prove reversal?** No.
- **What comparisons are most reliable?** Comparable instrument, venue, timeframe, and session data.

## Common mistake
Using a single volume rule regardless of price location or market condition.

## Risk note
Neither rising volume nor a lack of price progress guarantees the next direction.

## Evidence classification
Platform documentation and professional observation
