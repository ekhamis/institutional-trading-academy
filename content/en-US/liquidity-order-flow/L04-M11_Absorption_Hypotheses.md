# L04-M11 - Absorption Hypotheses

## Objective
Evaluate absorption hypotheses using reproducible observations and a conditional risk decision.

## Worked synthetic example
700 ask-classified contracts trade while the high advances only one tick; 100 contracts traded in the comparison window.

## Interpretation and alternatives
- Aggressive volume with limited price progress is consistent with absorption, but does not establish a hidden seller or reversal.
- Compare equal-duration windows, volatility, trade classification and price location. Resting size alone cannot establish absorption.
- Wait for a defined structural response; invalidate the reversal hypothesis on accepted continuation above the test.
- All numeric examples are synthetic and must not be interpreted as historical performance.

## Practice
Recalculate the worked case, state one alternative explanation, and submit a decision-time record with data settings, confirmation, invalidation, costs and a justified no-trade condition.

## Answer rationale
- What does the worked case establish? Aggressive volume with limited price progress is consistent with absorption, but does not establish a hidden seller or reversal.
- What cancels or limits the interpretation? Wait for a defined structural response; invalidate the reversal hypothesis on accepted continuation above the test.
- Does this example establish a profitable strategy? No. It is a synthetic reasoning exercise; an edge requires separate cost-aware evaluation.
