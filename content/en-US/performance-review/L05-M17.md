# L05-M17 - Drawdown and Recovery

## Objective
Apply drawdown and recovery to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
A synthetic equity series starts at 10000, peaks at 12000 and falls to 9000.

## Concept
Measure drawdown from a prior peak, not the starting deposit. Recovery uses the smaller current balance as denominator.

![Drawdown and Recovery](../../../assets/illustrations/097_review_17.png)

## Practice
Calculate peak-to-trough drawdown and the percentage gain needed to regain the peak. Ignore deposits and withdrawals in this example.

## Answer rationale
(12000 - 9000) / 12000 = 25% drawdown. 3000 / 9000 = 33.33% recovery gain.
