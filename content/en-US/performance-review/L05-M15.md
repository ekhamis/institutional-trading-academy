# L05-M15 - Journal Evidence and Missing Fills

## Objective
Apply journal evidence and missing fills to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
A chart box shows a target reached. The journal has no order confirmation, quantity or exit fill.

## Concept
Separate plan, submitted order, confirmed execution and claimed result. Preserve unknowns; do not turn annotations into audited performance.

![Journal Evidence and Missing Fills](../../../assets/illustrations/095_review_15.png)

## Practice
Create a record with timestamp, instrument/feed, plan version, order IDs, fill quantities/prices and costs. Leave unsupported fields unknown.

## Answer rationale
This is an unverified outcome, not a realized winner. Missing evidence is a review task, not permission to invent fills.
