# L05-M11 - Partial Exits and Weighted R

## Objective
Apply partial exits and weighted r to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
A simulated position starts with $100 planned risk. Half exits at +1R and half at +3R. Total costs are $12.

## Concept
Weight every exit by its fraction of the original quantity. Keep the original risk denominator.

![Partial Exits and Weighted R](../../../assets/illustrations/091_review_11.png)

## Practice
Compute gross and net R; compare with exiting all at +3R without assuming that target always fills.

## Answer rationale
0.5 x 1 + 0.5 x 3 = 2 gross R; ($200 - $12) / $100 = 1.88 net R.
