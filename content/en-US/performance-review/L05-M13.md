# L05-M13 - Breakeven Is Not Risk-Free

## Objective
Apply breakeven is not risk-free to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
A simulated long fills at 100 and its stop moves to 100. The exit fills at 99.8; quantity is 50 and total costs are $5.

## Concept
A stop trigger at entry does not guarantee a flat net result. Trailing rules change the payoff distribution and require testing.

![Breakeven Is Not Risk-Free](../../../assets/illustrations/093_review_13.png)

## Practice
Calculate net P/L and explain why moving a stop cannot be called removing all risk.

## Answer rationale
(99.8 - 100) x 50 - 5 = -$15. Gaps, slippage and costs remain.
