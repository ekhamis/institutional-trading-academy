# L05-M16 - Expectancy and Sample Uncertainty

## Objective
Apply expectancy and sample uncertainty to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
Ten synthetic outcomes contain four +2R wins and six -1R losses before costs. Modeled costs total 1R.

## Concept
Sample mean is an estimate, not proof of future edge. Dependence, changing conditions and selective reporting can invalidate simple confidence arguments.

![Expectancy and Sample Uncertainty](../../../assets/illustrations/096_review_16.png)

## Practice
Compute gross and net mean R. List missing evidence before accepting the strategy.

## Answer rationale
(4 x 2 - 6) / 10 = +0.2R gross per trade; (2 - 1) / 10 = +0.1R net. Ten outcomes do not establish a stable edge.
