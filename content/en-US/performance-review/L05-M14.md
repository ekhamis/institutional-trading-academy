# L05-M14 - Correlated Portfolio Scenarios

## Objective
Apply correlated portfolio scenarios to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
Two index-related simulated positions each have $100 planned risk. A common shock causes losses of $140 and $160.

## Concept
More tickers do not establish independent exposures. Evaluate common drivers and stressed combined loss; correlations are not fixed.

![Correlated Portfolio Scenarios](../../../assets/illustrations/094_review_14.png)

## Practice
Compare the sum of planned stop risks with the stated common-shock result. Do not infer a covariance matrix from this example.

## Answer rationale
$200 planned risk versus $300 scenario loss. The shock exceeds the planned sum by 50%.
