# L05-M20 - Performance Review Assessment

## Objective
Apply performance review assessment to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
Review an unseen synthetic journal containing partial exits, costs, a missing fill, correlated positions and an overstated result.

## Concept
Score arithmetic, evidence integrity, risk aggregation, uncertainty, test separation and remediation from 0 to 2. Require 10/12 and no critical failure.

![Performance Review Assessment](../../../assets/illustrations/100_review_20.png)

## Practice
Submit calculations, corrected unknowns, a stress scenario and a reproducible evaluation plan. A fabricated fill, hidden cost or leaked test is a critical failure.

## Answer rationale
Advance only after the rubric passes and all critical failures are repaired on a new case. This is an internal educational assessment, not accreditation.
