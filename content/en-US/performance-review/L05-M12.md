# L05-M12 - Precommitted Time Stops

## Objective
Apply precommitted time stops to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
A synthetic plan requires a review after six completed five-minute bars. Price has not reached its trigger at that time.

## Concept
Distinguish an entry expiry from a time-based exit of an existing position. Define the clock, timezone and exact rule before replay.

![Precommitted Time Stops](../../../assets/illustrations/092_review_12.png)

## Practice
Write separate expiry and filled-position exit rules. Mark which applies to this unfilled case.

## Answer rationale
The entry plan expires after 30 minutes under this exercise rule. There is no filled position to exit.
