# L05-M19 - Held-Out Testing Without Leakage

## Objective
Apply held-out testing without leakage to a reproducible decision record.

## Synthetic case
A researcher tunes rules on January-June, evaluates July-August, then repeatedly changes rules after viewing September-October test results.

## Concept
A test set used for tuning is no longer untouched. Split chronologically, preserve configurations and account for overlapping labels or forward-looking features.

![Held-Out Testing Without Leakage](../../../assets/illustrations/099_review_19.png)

## Practice
Design training, validation and final held-out periods. Explain how contaminated results must be labeled.

## Answer rationale
September-October became development data. Freeze a new specification and evaluate on new untouched data; do not relabel the old test as independent evidence.
