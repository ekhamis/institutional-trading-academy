# L02-M08 - Breakout Acceptance and Failure

**Estimated time:** 32 minutes

## Objective
Separate accepted breakouts from failed breakouts.

## Why it matters
Breakout traders and reversal traders often fight around the same level. The professional skill is not predicting every break, but reading whether price is accepted beyond the level.

## Core ideas
- Acceptance means price can hold beyond the broken area and continue doing business there.
- Failure means price breaks out but returns quickly into the prior range or structure.
- Failed breakouts can trap traders who entered late.
- Volume, volatility, session timing, and follow-through can help judge breakout quality.

## Scenario
US100 breaks above a pre-market high before the cash open, stalls, then falls back into the range. The breakout trader must recognize failure quickly instead of hoping.

## Practice
Find one accepted breakout and one failed breakout. Mark the point where a professional would stop trusting the breakout.

## Knowledge check
- **What is breakout acceptance?**
  - Holding and continuing beyond the broken area.
- **What is breakout failure?**
  - Returning quickly back into the prior area.
- **Why are failed breakouts important?**
  - They can trap late traders and reverse sharply.

## Common mistake
Assuming a level break is enough without acceptance.

## Evidence classification
Professional observation
