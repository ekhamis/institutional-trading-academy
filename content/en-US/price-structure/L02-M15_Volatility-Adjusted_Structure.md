# L02-M15 - Volatility-Adjusted Structure

**Estimated time:** 34 minutes

## Objective
Adjust structure expectations to the current volatility environment.

## Why it matters
A pullback that is normal during high volatility may be abnormal during a quiet session. Structure cannot be read well without understanding the scale of recent movement.

## Core ideas
- Volatility describes movement size, not direction.
- Structural breaks in high volatility need more room and stricter confirmation.
- Quiet markets require patience because small breaks can be noise.
- A level's importance should be interpreted relative to recent range size.

## Scenario
US30 moves 150 points in minutes after news. The same stop distance used during quiet pre-market conditions is no longer structurally comparable.

## Practice
Compare one quiet session and one high-volatility session. Note how swing size, wick size, and invalidation distance differ.

## Knowledge check
- **Does volatility indicate direction?**
  - No.
- **Why adjust structure for volatility?**
  - Because normal movement size changes.
- **What happens if risk ignores volatility?**
  - Stops and targets may become structurally meaningless.

## Common mistake
Using the same structural expectations in every volatility regime.

## Evidence classification
Widely accepted professional practice
