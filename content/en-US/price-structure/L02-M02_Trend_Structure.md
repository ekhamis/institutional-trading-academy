# L02-M02 - Trend Structure

**Estimated time:** 28 minutes

## Objective
Read trend through structural progression rather than through a single indicator line.

## Why it matters
Beginners often call a market bullish because one candle is green. Professional trend reading asks whether buyers or sellers keep defending increasingly favorable prices.

## Core ideas
- An uptrend is generally a sequence of higher highs and higher lows.
- A downtrend is generally a sequence of lower lows and lower highs.
- Healthy trends show continuation and controlled pullbacks.
- A trend is weakened when the side in control fails to defend the prior structural point.

## Scenario
US100 rallies strongly, pulls back, then fails to break the prior high. A trader should not simply assume the trend is intact; the quality of continuation must be evaluated.

## Practice
Mark the last three major highs and lows on US30, DAX40, and Gold. Classify each as uptrend, downtrend, or unclear.

## Knowledge check
- **What defines an uptrend structurally?**
  - Higher highs and higher lows.
- **What weakens a trend?**
  - Failure to continue or failure to defend the prior structural point.
- **Can a trend be unclear?**
  - Yes.

## Common mistake
Calling one strong candle a trend.

## Evidence classification
Widely accepted professional practice
