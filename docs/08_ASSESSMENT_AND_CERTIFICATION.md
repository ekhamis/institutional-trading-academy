# Assessment and Certification

## Assessment Types
Micro quiz, concept check, chart labeling, scenario decision, risk calculation, written plan, simulation, post-trade review, AI interview, capstone.

## Rules
Watching content is not mastery. High-risk modules require prerequisite passes. Failed assessments trigger remediation missions.

## Certificate Requirements
Theory pass, practical pass, risk pass, reflection/journal evidence, and refreshers for time-sensitive material.
