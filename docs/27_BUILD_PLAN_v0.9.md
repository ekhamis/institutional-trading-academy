## Acceptance criteria

- Research exchange and platform primary documentation; record exact references and dates. Practitioner hypotheses must be labelled and tested, not presented as established profitable methods.
- Each lesson includes objective, prerequisites, worked numeric example, alternative explanation, exercise, answer rationale, invalidation and no-trade case.
- Create distinct, topic-specific diagrams. Label synthetic examples and do not imply historical market provenance.
- Update canonical data to 80 missions, schema references, curriculum counts, localization status, knowledge graph and cumulative handbook source consistently.
- Keep Arabic content deferred until English lock. Preserve historical handbooks with their correct scope.
- Validator must check all prerequisites and source/asset references; add coverage for cycles and manifest consistency if absent.
- Review generated artifacts visually; run repository validation and CI before release readiness.
- Commit coherent increments to build/v0.9.0 and use a review PR. Keep VERSION at 0.8.0 until the actual candidate meets the gates above.
