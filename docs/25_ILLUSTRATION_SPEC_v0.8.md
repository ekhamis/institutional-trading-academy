# Illustration Specification v0.8.0 - Liquidity and Order Flow

## Shared production rules

- Export 1600 x 900 PNG files in the established dark academy style.
- Use synthetic, non-live market data and do not imply a trade recommendation or performance result.
- Label displayed orders, executed trades, estimates, and interpretation separately.
- Use color plus words or shapes so meaning is not color-only.
- Include the footer `Educational example - not a trading signal`.
- Number files sequentially 57 through 66.

## 57 - Liquidity dimensions
Show spread, five-level depth, and cost-to-trade for two order sizes. Make volume and depth separate panels.

## 58 - Limit-order-book queue
Show bids and asks by price with anonymous queue blocks, market-by-price aggregation, and market-by-order detail. State that matching rules vary.

## 59 - DOM moving snapshot
Show three sequential DOM states with additions, reductions, executions, and cancellations. Emphasize time and feed status.

## 60 - Observable and inferred liquidity
Split observed displayed orders and executions from inferred chart zones and unknown hidden or stop interest.

## 61 - Sweep and post-event evidence
Show one reference level, a move through it, then two possible outcomes: acceptance and return. Do not label either as automatic buy or sell.

## 62 - Footprint methodology
Show bid and ask traded volume at price inside a bar, beside a method panel comparing direct bid/ask data with intrabar estimates.

## 63 - Delta and price result
Show the same positive delta with progress, stall, and rejection outcomes. State that delta sign is not direction.

## 64 - CVD anchor and reset
Show one delta sequence accumulated under different reset anchors, plus a data-integrity checklist.

## 65 - Heatmap order changes
Show displayed liquidity bands over time with add, reduce, execute, and cancel markers. Separate observable changes from intent claims.

## 66 - Assessment worksheet
Show a data-provenance and evidence-classification workflow leading to conditional plan, unresolved, or no trade with equal visual weight.
