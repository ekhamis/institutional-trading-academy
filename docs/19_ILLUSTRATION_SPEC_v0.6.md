# Illustration Specification v0.6.0 - Volume and Auction, Set 1

## Shared production rules

- Export 1600 x 900 PNG files in the established dark academy style.
- Use synthetic, unlabeled price data; do not imply a real trade recommendation or performance result.
- Keep labels readable at handbook scale and use color plus shape or pattern so meaning is not color-only.
- Include a small footer: `Educational example - not a trading signal`.
- Number files sequentially after the current illustration set.

## 37 - Volume measures activity, not direction

**Filename:** `37_volume_activity_not_direction.png`

One price panel above one volume panel. Highlight three equally large volume bars beneath three different outcomes: continuation, rejection back into a range, and two-sided balance. Label the volume fact separately from each price response. The learner takeaway is that activity is observed while direction remains contextual.

## 38 - Price result versus volume effort

**Filename:** `38_price_volume_effort_result.png`

Two side-by-side chart fragments using the same volume height. Panel A shows wide price progress; Panel B shows narrow progress at a marked resistance zone. Add an `activity` arrow to both volume bars and distinct `price result` brackets above. Do not label either panel as a buy or sell.

## 39 - Relative volume baseline

**Filename:** `39_relative_volume_baseline.png`

Show current volume, a ten-period average baseline, and the ratio `current / average = 1.8`. Add a small comparison inset showing how a different lookback changes the baseline. State that `above 1.0 = above the selected average`, not bullish.

## 40 - Session participation curve

**Filename:** `40_session_volume_participation.png`

Display five simplified sessions aligned by time, plus a typical U-shaped participation curve for the regular session. Compare a current opening bar with prior opening bars, not with midday bars. Mark pre-open and regular-session boundaries and note that venue and feed rules can differ.

## 41 - VWAP foundations

**Filename:** `41_vwap_foundations.png`

Show an intraday price path, session-reset VWAP line, and volume bars. Include a compact formula callout describing a cumulative volume-weighted average and a clear anchor/reset marker. Label price above and below VWAP as location only. Add `not guaranteed support or resistance` beside the line.

## Review checklist

- Each diagram teaches one observable comparison.
- All labels match L03-M01 through L03-M05 terminology.
- No arrow or color implies guaranteed direction.
- Instrument, session, provider, and anchor limitations are visible where relevant.
- Filenames are assigned to draft `visual` fields only after the PNGs exist.
