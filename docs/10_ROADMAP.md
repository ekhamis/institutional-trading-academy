# Roadmap

## Current build increment

v0.8.0 begins Level 4, Liquidity and Order Flow, with spread and depth, limit-order-book structure, DOM, observable versus inferred liquidity, post-sweep evidence, footprint methodology, delta, cumulative delta, heatmaps, data limitations, and a practical assessment. The authorized scope and acceptance criteria are defined in `docs/23_BUILD_PLAN_v0.8.md`.

## Next curriculum increment

The next English-first increment should deepen Level 4 with absorption and exhaustion tests, stacked imbalances, order-flow divergence, book resiliency, replay methodology, advanced no-trade conditions, and a Level 4 completion assessment. The exact mission count and version remain subject to validation of the v0.8.0 candidate.

## Phase 1
Lock charter, MVP curriculum, first 10 missions, illustration standards, clickable prototype.

## Phase 2
Complete the English master, exercises, quizzes, glossaries, and review gates. Arabic authoring begins only after English v1.0.0 lock.

## Phase 3

## Phase 4
Trading Lab, chart scenarios, replay, AI review, practical assessments.

## Phase 5
Mobile app, community, advanced certification, institutional licensing.
