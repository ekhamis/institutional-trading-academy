# Curriculum Blueprint

## Level 0 — Financial Literacy
Money, inflation, debt, equity, leverage, compounding, investing vs trading.

## Level 1 — Market Foundations
Exchanges, brokers, market makers, order types, bid/ask, spread, sessions.

## Level 2 — Price and Structure
Trend, range, swing points, support/resistance, BOS, CHoCH, multi-timeframe analysis.

## Level 3 — Volume and Auction
Volume, volume profile, POC, VAH, VAL, HVN, LVN, auction theory, VWAP.

## Level 4 — Liquidity and Order Flow
Liquidity pools, sweeps, footprint, delta, CVD, absorption, DOM, heatmaps.

## Level 5 — Planning and Execution
Setup qualification, entries, invalidation, stops, targets, management, R-multiples, no-trade decisions.

## Level 6 — Psychology and Performance
FOMO, revenge trading, overtrading, habits, journaling, expectancy, drawdown, recovery.

## Level 7 — Specializations
- Indices: US30, US100, US500, UK100, DAX40
- Gold
- Forex
- Equities and ETFs: top liquid stocks, SPY, QQQ, IWM
- Funded accounts

## Level 8 — Certification
Market Analyst, Risk Manager, Index Trader, Gold Trader, Forex Trader, Equity/ETF Analyst, Funded Account Specialist.
