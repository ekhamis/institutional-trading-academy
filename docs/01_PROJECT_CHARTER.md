# Project Charter

## Vision
Build a bilingual market-intelligence and trading education platform that takes a complete beginner from financial literacy to independent, evidence-based market analysis and disciplined execution.

## Mission
Teach how markets work, how to make decisions under uncertainty, and how to improve through structured practice, simulation, reflection, and feedback.

## Non-goals
- No signal service
- No guaranteed-profit claims
- No dependence on a single guru
- No promotion of excessive leverage
- No certification based only on watching videos

## Core Principles
1. Process before profit
2. Risk before reward
3. Evidence before opinion
4. Practice before certification
5. Mastery before progression
6. Visual before verbose
7. Independence before dependence
8. Transparent uncertainty
9. Bilingual by design
10. Mobile-first and accessible
